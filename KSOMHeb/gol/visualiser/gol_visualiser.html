Game of Life — CPAF substrate #2 explorer

A cellular automaton as a testbed for CPAF's basic-layer vocabulary. Draw cells, stamp patterns, run the rule (B3/S23), then hit Detect entities: the tool finds connected components and classifies each by evolving it in isolation — its period and velocity. That isolation test is the operational meaning of an entity (a self-determined, recurring pattern) and of closure (its future needs only its own cells). See ../CONCEPT_MAPPING.md.
